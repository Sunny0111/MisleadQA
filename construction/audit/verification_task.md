# MisleadQA reviewer guide

You will review **50 items**. Work independently. Do not discuss items with
other reviewers or search online or in outside materials.

Enter your own reviewer ID assigned by the organizer, A1–A6. You will see a
question, five options, the gold and lure answers, the target and alternate
scopes, and three passages: G, S, and F.

**Scope** means the entity, time, location, or condition being described.
**Gold** is the supplied answer for the target scope. **Lure** is the alternative
answer used in S and F. Treat the underlying source facts as given; the task
is to check what the passages say and whether their answers and scopes match.
F may deliberately state a false answer. Judge the text, not just its label.

For every item, answer:

**Q1 · Passage G**  
Does G clearly support the gold answer in the target scope?

**Q2 · Passage S**  
Does S clearly support the lure answer in a different scope from G?  
Only one scope element should differ: entity, time, location, or condition.

S must clearly state the answer to the same attribute in another scope.
Missing information or vague wording alone is not a clear change of scope.

**Q3 · Passage F**  
Does F clearly support the lure answer in the same scope as G?  
F should match G except for replacing the gold answer with the lure answer.

Check for other edits, a leftover gold answer, or broken grammar.

Choose **Yes** only if the whole check is met. If a check fails or you cannot
tell, choose **No**. Explain the affected question in the note and select
**Clear issue** or **Unsure**. If both apply, choose Unsure and explain both.

Drafts save automatically on the computer running the local app. Wait for
**Saved locally** before closing the page. If saving fails, keep the page open
and retry, or download a recovery copy. You can resume with the same reviewer
ID on the same computer. You may edit answers before final submission.

At the last item, submit once all 50 items are complete. The app shows the
saved JSON file path. Submitted answers are locked; you can still view them
and download a copy. Contact the organizer if a submitted record needs a
correction; do not edit the frozen items or the submitted file yourself.
